# Third-party asset attribution

## Nia research guide

- **Character:** Casual Female, from Quaternius's Ultimate Animated Characters pack.
- **Local asset:** `public/models/casual_female.glb`.
- **Download source:** [Casual Female GLB](https://cdn.cinevva.com/assets/packs/quaternius/ultimate-animated-characters/Casual_Female.glb?download=1).
- **Asset and license confirmation:** [Cinevva's free character and animation guide](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/free-character-animations-rigging) identifies this character and its 17 embedded clips as CC0.
- **Quaternius license information:** [Universal Animation Library](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html) states CC0 and permits personal, educational, and commercial use.
- **License text:** [Creative Commons CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The model is stored locally and needs no external textures or runtime asset service. `src/nia3d.js` adjusts its material colors, adds a geometric research tablet, lighting and a shadow, and plays the model's embedded `Idle` animation. Nia is a presentation character, not a conversational AI or a claim about a real researcher.

The Willow Grid screenshot in `public/images/willow-grid.png` is a capture of the actual DYNA laboratory. The scientific interface diagrams are local project assets.
